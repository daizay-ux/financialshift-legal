# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: May 21, 2026**

## 1. Introduction

FinancialShift ("we", "our", "us") operates the FinancialShift YouTube channel (@FinancialShift) and the FinancialShift Auto Post application ("the App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use the App or interact with our content.

Contact: jonathan.gre13@gmail.com

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to:
- The FinancialShift Auto Post application that publishes pre-produced video content to social media platforms (YouTube, TikTok, Instagram) on behalf of the channel owner.
- The FinancialShift YouTube channel and its viewers.

The App is a personal automation tool operated by the channel owner. It does not have public users, does not collect data from third parties, and does not display advertising.

## 3. Information We Collect

### 3.1 OAuth Authentication Tokens
When the channel owner authenticates the App with YouTube, TikTok, or Instagram, the App stores OAuth access and refresh tokens locally on the operator's machine. These tokens are never transmitted to any third-party server.

### 3.2 Video Metadata
The App stores metadata about videos it uploads (title, description, tags, scheduled publish time, platform IDs) in local JSON files on the operator's machine.

### 3.3 No End-User Data Collection
The App does not collect data from YouTube/TikTok/Instagram viewers. Viewer analytics shown to the channel owner come directly from the platforms' native dashboards.

## 4. How We Use Information

- OAuth tokens are used solely to authenticate API requests to YouTube, TikTok, and Instagram for the purpose of uploading and scheduling the channel's own video content.
- Video metadata is used to track which content has been published, to which platform, and at what time, to avoid duplicate uploads and to coordinate cross-platform release timing.

## 5. Data Storage and Security

- All sensitive data (OAuth tokens, API keys) is stored locally on the operator's Windows machine in protected files.
- No data is transmitted to any FinancialShift-controlled server. The App has no backend service.
- Communication with YouTube, TikTok, and Instagram APIs occurs over HTTPS.

## 6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties. The only external services contacted by the App are the official APIs of:
- YouTube (Google LLC)
- TikTok (TikTok Pte. Ltd.)
- Instagram (Meta Platforms, Inc.)

Each of these services has its own privacy policy governing the data they receive and process.

## 7. Third-Party Services

The App relies on the following third-party services. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies:
- **YouTube Data API v3**: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- **TikTok Content Posting API**: https://www.tiktok.com/legal/privacy-policy
- **Instagram Graph API / Private API**: https://privacycenter.instagram.com/policy

## 8. Data Retention

- OAuth tokens are retained until revoked by the channel owner or expired by the platform.
- Video metadata is retained indefinitely for operational purposes (avoiding duplicate uploads).
- The operator can delete all local data at any time by removing the application files.

## 9. Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Because the App is a personal automation tool with no end-user accounts, GDPR and CCPA data subject rights apply primarily to the channel owner (the operator) and to identifiable information that may appear in published video content.

If you believe your personal data appears in our published content and you wish to request deletion, modification, or access, please contact jonathan.gre13@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

## 10. Children's Privacy

The FinancialShift channel and App are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13. Content on the channel is general financial education for adult viewers.

## 11. Cookies and Tracking
